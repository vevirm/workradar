What the research says about where work happens and whether it works. Scanned by hand from top-tier work and employment journals and from government and institutional publishers.

How to read this

Placement is a claim about the paper's own language, not a judgement about whether the paper is right. Every placed item shows the sentence it was placed on, so you can disagree with it. Items the scanner cannot place honestly are kept in the holding bay rather than pushed into a quadrant, and a curator can pin or withdraw any placement.
